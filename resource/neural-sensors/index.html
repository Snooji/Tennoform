Neural Sensors

Resource

Found on: Jupiter.

Best place to farm it: Io, Jupiter (Defense).

Jupiter, Kuva Fortress. Taveuni (Kuva Fortress Survival) late game.

Early game

  • Elara, Jupiter (Survival, level 15–20): Endless; resource drops pile up over time
  • Io, Jupiter (Defense, level 15–20): Endless waves; stand still and loot
  • Callisto, Jupiter (Interception, level 15–20): Endless; good with loot frames

Mid game

  • Sinai, Jupiter (Defense, level 20–30): Dark Sector +20% resources. Endless waves; stand still and loot
  • Cameria, Jupiter (Survival, level 20–30): Dark Sector +20% resources. Endless; resource drops pile up over time
  • Nabuk, Kuva Fortress (Defense, level 30–32): Endless waves; stand still and loot

Late game

  • Tamu, Kuva Fortress (Disruption, level 35–40): Endless with high drop rate
