Gallium

Resource

Found on: Mars and Uranus.

Best place to farm it: Ophelia, Uranus (Survival).

Mars and Uranus. Uranus bosses (Tyl Regor) also drop it.

Early game

  • Kadesh, Mars (Defense, level 10–20): Dark Sector +20% resources. Endless waves; stand still and loot
  • Wahiba, Mars (Survival, level 10–20): Dark Sector +20% resources. Endless; resource drops pile up over time
  • Spear, Mars (Defense, level 8–13): Endless waves; stand still and loot

Mid game

  • Assur, Uranus (Survival, level 25–35): Dark Sector +25% resources. Endless; resource drops pile up over time
  • Stephano, Uranus (Defense, level 24–29): Endless waves; stand still and loot
  • Ophelia, Uranus (Survival, level 24–29): Endless; resource drops pile up over time
