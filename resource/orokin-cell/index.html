Orokin Cell

Resource

Found on: Ceres, Saturn, and Deimos.

Best place to farm it: Helene, Saturn (Defense).

Saturn, Ceres, Deimos. Gabii (Ceres) and Lephantis (Orokin Derelict assassinate) too.

Early game

  • Seimeni, Ceres (Defense, level 15–25): Dark Sector +35% resources. Endless waves; stand still and loot
  • Gabii, Ceres (Survival, level 15–25): Dark Sector +35% resources. Endless; resource drops pile up over time
  • Casta, Ceres (Defense, level 12–17): Endless waves; stand still and loot

Mid game

  • Piscinas, Saturn (Survival, level 26–36): Dark Sector +20% resources. Endless; resource drops pile up over time
  • Helene, Saturn (Defense, level 21–26): Endless waves; stand still and loot
  • Titan, Saturn (Survival, level 21–26): Endless; resource drops pile up over time

Late game

  • Persto, Deimos (Survival, level 55–60): Endless; resource drops pile up over time
  • Armatus, Deimos (Disruption, level 55–60): Endless with high drop rate
  • Abyssal Zone, Ceres (Exterminate, level 40–45): Fast, lots of kills
