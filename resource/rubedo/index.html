Rubedo

Resource

Found on: Earth, Lua, Phobos, Europa, Pluto, Sedna, and the Void.

Best place to farm it: Hieracon, Pluto (Excavation).

Also Void tiles (Mot). Rubedo drops in crates. Use loot radar.

Early game

  • Zeugma, Phobos (Survival, level 15–25): Dark Sector +25% resources. Endless; resource drops pile up over time
  • Coba, Earth (Defense, level 6–16): Dark Sector +15% resources. Endless waves; stand still and loot
  • Tikal, Earth (Excavation, level 6–16): Dark Sector +15% resources. Endless; excavators also drop resource caches

Mid game

  • Larzac, Europa (Defense, level 23–33): Dark Sector +25% resources. Endless waves; stand still and loot
  • Cholistan, Europa (Excavation, level 23–33): Dark Sector +25% resources. Endless; excavators also drop resource caches
  • Ani, Void (Survival, level 20–25): Endless; resource drops pile up over time

Late game

  • Hieracon, Pluto (Excavation, level 35–45): Dark Sector +35% resources. Endless; excavators also drop resource caches
  • Sechura, Pluto (Defense, level 35–45): Dark Sector +35% resources. Endless waves; stand still and loot
  • Amarna, Sedna (Survival, level 35–45): Dark Sector +25% resources. Endless; resource drops pile up over time

Drop chances

  • Venus/Orb Vallis (Level 20 - 40 Orb Vallis Bounty) · Rot C: 25%
  • Void/Marduk (Caches) · Rot B: 15.2%
