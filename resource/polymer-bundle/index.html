Resource

Found on: Mercury, Venus, and Uranus.

Best place to farm it: Ophelia, Uranus (Survival).

Mercury, Venus and Uranus. Ophelia also gives Gallium and Plastids.

Early game

  • Romula, Venus (Defense, level 8–18): Dark Sector +10% resources. Endless waves; stand still and loot
  • Malva, Venus (Survival, level 8–18): Dark Sector +10% resources. Endless; resource drops pile up over time
  • Kiliken, Venus (Excavation, level 3–8): Endless; excavators also drop resource caches

Mid game

  • Assur, Uranus (Survival, level 25–35): Dark Sector +25% resources. Endless; resource drops pile up over time
  • Stephano, Uranus (Defense, level 24–29): Endless waves; stand still and loot
  • Ophelia, Uranus (Survival, level 24–29): Endless; resource drops pile up over time
