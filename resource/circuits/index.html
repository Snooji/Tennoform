Circuits

Resource

Found on: Venus, Ceres, and the Kuva Fortress.

Best place to farm it: Gabii, Ceres (Dark Sector Survival).

Ceres, Venus, Void, Kuva Fortress.

Early game

  • Seimeni, Ceres (Defense, level 15–25): Dark Sector +35% resources. Endless waves; stand still and loot
  • Gabii, Ceres (Survival, level 15–25): Dark Sector +35% resources. Endless; resource drops pile up over time
  • Romula, Venus (Defense, level 8–18): Dark Sector +10% resources. Endless waves; stand still and loot

Mid game

  • Nabuk, Kuva Fortress (Defense, level 30–32): Endless waves; stand still and loot
  • Taveuni, Kuva Fortress (Survival, level 32–37): Endless; resource drops pile up over time
  • Deepmines, Venus (Free Roam, level 30–40): Open world, mine and loot

Late game

  • Tamu, Kuva Fortress (Disruption, level 35–40): Endless with high drop rate
  • Abyssal Zone, Ceres (Exterminate, level 40–45): Fast, lots of kills

Drop chances

  • Venus/Orb Vallis (Level 20 - 40 Orb Vallis Bounty) · Rot A: 25%
  • Earth/Cetus (Level 10 - 30 Cetus Bounty) · Rot C: 25%
