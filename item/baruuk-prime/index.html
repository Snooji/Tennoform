Baruuk Prime

Warframe · Prime · Vaulted

How to get Baruuk Prime

  • Blueprint comes from Axi B9 relic (rare), Lith B10 relic (rare), Lith B11 relic (rare), Meso B8 relic (rare), Meso B9 relic (rare).
  • It is vaulted: its relics no longer drop, so get them from Prime Resurgence or by trading.

Parts and resources

  • Baruuk Prime Neuroptics Blueprint: relics Axi B5 (rare), Axi B6 (rare), Axi B7 (rare), Axi B8 (rare), Axi B9 (uncommon), Neo B9 (rare)
  • Baruuk Prime Chassis Blueprint: relics Lith N15 (uncommon), Lith P8 (uncommon), Lith T13 (uncommon), Meso V9 (uncommon), Neo A8 (uncommon), Neo K7 (uncommon)
  • Baruuk Prime Systems Blueprint: relics Axi P7 (uncommon), Axi W3 (uncommon), Meso H4 (uncommon), Meso H6 (uncommon), Meso H7 (uncommon), Meso P14 (uncommon)
  • Orokin Cell ×5

Building it in the Foundry costs 25,000 credits and takes 3 days.

Stats

  • Health: 180
  • Shields: 365
  • Armor: 240
  • Energy: 200
  • Sprint speed: 1.2

Abilities

  • Elude: Dodge all incoming projectiles, but only while not attacking. Use again to deactivate this ability.
  • Lull: A calming wave slows enemies until they fall into a slumber. Enemies woken by damage will be confused and disoriented. Short-term amnesia means all waking enemies forget anything that happened before the lull.
  • Desolate Hands: Summon a bevy of orbiting daggers to seek out enemy guns, destroying them with a small explosion. Combine with Elude to double the range.
  • Serene Storm: With his Restraint eroded, Baruuk commands the Desert Wind to deliver powerful radial strikes with his fists and feet. Each moment commanding the storm restores his Restraint.
