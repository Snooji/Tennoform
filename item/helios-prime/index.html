Helios Prime

Sentinel · Prime · Vaulted

How to get Helios Prime

  • Blueprint comes from Lith C3 relic (uncommon), Lith N2 relic (uncommon), Lith V4 relic (uncommon), Meso C3 relic (uncommon), Meso E5 relic (uncommon), Meso Z1 relic (uncommon), Neo K1 relic (uncommon), Neo M1 relic (uncommon).
  • It is vaulted: its relics no longer drop, so get them from Prime Resurgence or by trading.

Parts and resources

  • Helios Prime Blueprint: relics Lith C3 (uncommon), Lith N2 (uncommon), Lith V4 (uncommon), Meso C3 (uncommon), Meso E5 (uncommon), Meso Z1 (uncommon)
  • Helios Prime Carapace: relics Axi A3 (uncommon), Axi C3 (uncommon), Axi H3 (uncommon), Lith K5 (uncommon), Lith P1 (uncommon), Meso E5 (uncommon)
  • Helios Prime Cerebrum: relics Axi H1 (rare), Axi H2 (rare), Axi H5 (rare), Lith H1 (rare), Lith H2 (rare), Meso H1 (rare)
  • Orokin Cell ×6
  • Helios Prime Systems: relics Axi A12 (uncommon), Axi N5 (uncommon), Axi O2 (uncommon), Lith V3 (uncommon), Lith Z2 (uncommon), Meso D2 (uncommon)

Building it in the Foundry costs 15,000 credits and takes 24 hours.

Stats

  • Health: 700
  • Shields: 300
  • Armor: 100
  • Energy: 100
