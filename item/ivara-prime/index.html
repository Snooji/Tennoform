Ivara Prime

Warframe · Prime · Vaulted

How to get Ivara Prime

  • Blueprint comes from Axi B4 relic (uncommon), Axi S13 relic (uncommon), Lith B12 relic (uncommon), Lith B8 relic (uncommon), Lith N4 relic (uncommon), Lith N5 relic (uncommon), Lith N7 relic (uncommon), Meso N9 relic (uncommon).
  • It is vaulted: its relics no longer drop, so get them from Prime Resurgence or by trading.

Parts and resources

  • Ivara Prime Neuroptics Blueprint: relics Lith D3 (uncommon), Meso B5 (uncommon), Meso C4 (uncommon), Meso D6 (uncommon), Meso N10 (uncommon), Meso O6 (uncommon)
  • Ivara Prime Chassis Blueprint: relics Lith I1 (rare), Meso I3 (rare), Neo I1 (rare), Neo I2 (rare), Neo I3 (rare)
  • Ivara Prime Systems Blueprint: relics Axi A15 (uncommon), Axi A9 (uncommon), Axi C6 (uncommon), Lith A4 (uncommon), Lith T7 (uncommon), Meso P2 (uncommon)
  • Orokin Cell ×5

Building it in the Foundry costs 25,000 credits and takes 3 days.

Stats

  • Health: 180
  • Shields: 455
  • Armor: 135
  • Energy: 250
  • Sprint speed: 1.2

Abilities

  • Quiver: Cycle through and shoot one of four tactical arrows. Cloak creates a stationary bubble that cloaks Ivara and allies. Dashwire creates a traversable zipline. Noise emits a high-pitched sound that attracts enemies to it. Sleep temporarily puts nearby enemies into a deep slumber.
  • Navigator: Assume control of any projectile launched by Ivara and guide it to the target.
  • Prowl: Become invisible and steal loot from unsuspecting enemies or take out prey with deadly headshots.
  • Artemis Bow: Summon a mighty bow and unleash a volley of devastating arrows.
