Destreza Prime

Melee · Prime · Mastery rank 10 · Vaulted

How to get Destreza Prime

  • Blueprint comes from Axi D3 relic (uncommon), Axi K4 relic (uncommon), Axi P2 relic (uncommon), Lith M4 relic (uncommon), Lith P7 relic (uncommon), Meso R2 relic (uncommon), Neo B5 relic (uncommon), Neo S9 relic (uncommon).
  • It is vaulted: its relics no longer drop, so get them from Prime Resurgence or by trading.

Parts and resources

  • Destreza Prime Blade: relics Axi D1 (rare), Axi D2 (rare), Lith D5 (rare), Meso D2 (rare), Meso D3 (rare), Neo D4 (rare)
  • Destreza Prime Handle: relics Axi D2 (uncommon), Axi M4 (uncommon), Axi O3 (uncommon), Axi O4 (uncommon), Axi R2 (uncommon), Lith L1 (uncommon)
  • Orokin Cell ×10

Building it in the Foundry costs 15,000 credits and takes 12 hours.
