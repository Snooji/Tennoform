Cobra & Crane Prime

Melee · Prime · Mastery rank 14 · Vaulted

How to get Cobra & Crane Prime

  • Blueprint comes from Axi B9 relic (uncommon), Axi K11 relic (uncommon), Lith G10 relic (uncommon), Lith N15 relic (uncommon), Meso K5 relic (uncommon), Neo S18 relic (uncommon).
  • It is vaulted: its relics no longer drop, so get them from Prime Resurgence or by trading.

Parts and resources

  • Cobra & Crane Prime Blade: relics Axi G9 (uncommon), Axi L6 (uncommon), Meso H6 (uncommon), Meso N14 (uncommon), Meso R6 (uncommon), Meso W5 (uncommon)
  • Cobra & Crane Prime Hilt: relics Axi A18 (uncommon), Axi H6 (uncommon), Axi H7 (uncommon), Lith A9 (uncommon), Lith G12 (uncommon), Lith H7 (uncommon)
  • Cobra & Crane Prime Guard: relics Axi C9 (rare), Lith C10 (rare), Lith C11 (rare), Lith C12 (rare), Meso C8 (rare), Neo C3 (rare)
  • Orokin Cell ×10

Building it in the Foundry costs 15,000 credits and takes 12 hours.
