Yareli Prime

Warframe · Prime

How to get Yareli Prime

  • Blueprint comes from Lith C15 relic (uncommon), Meso D8 relic (uncommon), Meso P17 relic (uncommon), Neo A14 relic (uncommon), Neo T11 relic (uncommon).

Parts and resources

  • Yareli Prime Neuroptics Blueprint: relics Axi Y2 (rare), Axi Y3 (rare), Lith Y1 (rare), Meso Y1 (rare), Meso Y2 (rare)
  • Yareli Prime Chassis Blueprint: relics Lith A10 (uncommon), Lith Z4 (uncommon), Meso K9 (uncommon), Meso X1 (uncommon), Neo A16 (uncommon), Neo V11 (uncommon)
  • Yareli Prime Systems Blueprint: relics Axi Y1 (rare), Neo Y1 (rare), Neo Y2 (rare)
  • Orokin Cell ×5

Building it in the Foundry costs 25,000 credits and takes 3 days.

Stats

  • Health: 270
  • Shields: 550
  • Armor: 105
  • Energy: 215
  • Sprint speed: 1.1

Abilities

  • Sea Snares: Form five water globules that seek out enemies and expand on contact, simultaneously damaging and immobilizing their victims. Enemies hit by the globules take increased damage from all sources.
  • Merulina: Summon Merulina, a rideable creature of the waves, and the inspiration for K-Driving. Merulina protects Yareli by absorbing a large portion of incoming damage.
  • Aquablades: Tear through foes with a trio of orbiting aquatic blades.
  • Riptide: Drag enemies into a crushing maelstrom and then blow them away in a watery burst. Each enemy trapped in the vortex increases the burst's damage.
