Akstiletto Prime

Secondary · Prime · Mastery rank 10 · Vaulted

How to get Akstiletto Prime

  • Blueprint comes from Lith A1 relic (rare), Lith A2 relic (rare), Neo A1 relic (rare), Neo A4 relic (rare), Neo A7 relic (rare).
  • It is vaulted: its relics no longer drop, so get them from Prime Resurgence or by trading.

Parts and resources

  • Akstiletto Prime Barrel ×2: relics Axi A1 (uncommon), Axi H2 (uncommon), Lith A5 (uncommon), Lith H1 (uncommon), Meso K1 (uncommon), Meso V6 (uncommon)
  • Akstiletto Prime Receiver ×2: relics Axi A7 (uncommon), Axi H1 (uncommon), Axi K1 (uncommon), Axi O1 (uncommon), Axi T1 (uncommon), Meso T6 (uncommon)
  • Akstiletto Prime Link: relics Axi N1 (uncommon), Lith V7 (uncommon), Meso O1 (uncommon), Neo D6 (uncommon), Neo N5 (uncommon)

Building it in the Foundry costs 15,000 credits and takes 12 hours.
