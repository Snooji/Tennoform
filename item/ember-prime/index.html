Ember Prime

Warframe · Prime · Vaulted

How to get Ember Prime

  • Blueprint comes from Axi E1 relic (rare), Lith E1 relic (rare), Meso E1 relic (rare), Neo E1 relic (rare).
  • It is vaulted: its relics no longer drop, so get them from Prime Resurgence or by trading.

Parts and resources

  • Ember Prime Neuroptics Blueprint: relics Meso F3 (uncommon), Meso S14 (uncommon), Neo S5 (uncommon)
  • Ember Prime Chassis Blueprint: relics Meso B10 (uncommon), Meso F2 (uncommon), Neo F1 (uncommon)
  • Ember Prime Systems Blueprint: relics Axi R4 (uncommon), Axi S2 (uncommon), Lith G1 (uncommon)
  • Orokin Cell

Building it in the Foundry costs 25,000 credits and takes 3 days.

Stats

  • Health: 270
  • Shields: 365
  • Armor: 160
  • Energy: 175
  • Sprint speed: 1.1

Abilities

  • Fireball: Release a fiery projectile that ignites enemies on contact.
  • Immolation: Protect Ember with flame armor that burns stronger over time, consuming energy once its meter is at full strength. Cast again to extinguish the flame.
  • Fire Blast: Slam the ground to create a wave of incinerating plasma that knocks back enemies and strips their armor.
  • Inferno: Command a flaming comet to crash down in front of Ember, engulfing enemies with a fire that can spread through their ranks.
