Hildryn Prime

Warframe · Prime · Vaulted

How to get Hildryn Prime

  • Blueprint comes from Axi H6 relic (rare), Axi H8 relic (rare), Meso H5 relic (rare), Meso H7 relic (rare), Neo H4 relic (rare).
  • It is vaulted: its relics no longer drop, so get them from Prime Resurgence or by trading.

Parts and resources

  • Hildryn Prime Neuroptics Blueprint: relics Axi W4 (uncommon), Lith G10 (uncommon), Lith M9 (uncommon), Meso P14 (uncommon), Neo C4 (uncommon), Neo P7 (uncommon)
  • Hildryn Prime Chassis Blueprint: relics Axi A18 (uncommon), Axi L6 (uncommon), Meso G10 (uncommon), Meso P11 (uncommon), Neo G7 (uncommon), Neo Q1 (uncommon)
  • Hildryn Prime Systems Blueprint: relics Axi P6 (uncommon), Axi S17 (uncommon), Axi S19 (uncommon), Axi T12 (uncommon), Lith A6 (uncommon), Lith H8 (uncommon)
  • Orokin Cell ×5

Building it in the Foundry costs 25,000 credits and takes 3 days.

Stats

  • Health: 270
  • Shields: 1380
  • Armor: 315
  • Sprint speed: 1.05

Abilities

  • Balefire: Charge and launch devastating bolts of electricity.
  • Pillage: Pillage a percentage of Shields and Armor of nearby enemies to replenish Hildryn's own Shields and Overshields. Also removes Status Effects from Hildryn and her allies.
  • Haven: Create a shield aura around allies. Enemies that approach Hildryn will take Radiation Damage.
  • Aegis Storm: Take the skies and rain Balefire rockets down on the enemy. Nearby enemies are blasted into the air where they will create an Energy Orb every few seconds. When shields run out the enemies are smashed into the ground.
