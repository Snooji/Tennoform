Wukong Prime

Warframe · Prime · Vaulted

How to get Wukong Prime

  • Blueprint comes from Axi W1 relic (rare), Axi W2 relic (rare), Lith W1 relic (rare), Lith W2 relic (rare), Meso W1 relic (rare), Meso W2 relic (rare).
  • It is vaulted: its relics no longer drop, so get them from Prime Resurgence or by trading.

Parts and resources

  • Wukong Prime Neuroptics Blueprint: relics Axi G2 (uncommon), Axi T4 (uncommon), Axi Z1 (uncommon), Lith D2 (uncommon), Lith S13 (uncommon), Neo I1 (uncommon)
  • Wukong Prime Chassis Blueprint: relics Axi A10 (uncommon), Axi T6 (uncommon), Lith K3 (uncommon), Lith N13 (uncommon), Meso B4 (uncommon), Meso K3 (uncommon)
  • Wukong Prime Systems Blueprint: relics Axi A11 (uncommon), Axi P3 (uncommon), Axi R3 (uncommon), Meso K2 (uncommon), Meso T4 (uncommon), Neo I2 (uncommon)
  • Orokin Cell ×5

Building it in the Foundry costs 25,000 credits and takes 3 days.

Stats

  • Health: 455
  • Shields: 345
  • Armor: 290
  • Energy: 145
  • Sprint speed: 1.05

Abilities

  • Celestial Twin: Shedding part of himself, Wukong creates a twin to fight by his side. Attack at range and the twin will melee, pull a blade and the twin will lay down covering fire. Use again to command the twin to attack a target with increased damage.
  • Cloud Walker: Evaporate into a cloud of mist and float through the battlefield, dazing any enemies encountered, while healing Wukong and his twin.
  • Defy: Wukong and his twin become invulnerable and defy enemies to attack. All damage is captured, stored, and dealt back in a single furious strike of Wukong's staff. Bonus Armor is then granted relative to the damage captured.
  • Primal Fury: Summon the iron staff and unleash fury.
