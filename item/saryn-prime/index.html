Saryn Prime

Warframe · Prime · Vaulted

How to get Saryn Prime

  • Blueprint comes from Axi N12 relic (uncommon), Axi N6 relic (uncommon), Lith S4 relic (rare), Lith V6 relic (uncommon), Meso C3 relic (uncommon), Meso S4 relic (rare), Neo S1 relic (rare).
  • It is vaulted: its relics no longer drop, so get them from Prime Resurgence or by trading.

Parts and resources

  • Saryn Prime Neuroptics Blueprint: relics Axi V9 (uncommon), Lith A1 (uncommon), Lith C5 (uncommon), Meso S5 (uncommon), Meso S9 (uncommon), Neo S19 (uncommon)
  • Saryn Prime Chassis Blueprint: relics Lith S17 (rare), Meso S2 (rare), Neo S10 (rare), Neo S13 (rare), Neo S2 (rare)
  • Saryn Prime Systems Blueprint: relics Axi G1 (uncommon), Axi S5 (uncommon), Axi T1 (uncommon), Lith H1 (uncommon), Meso C1 (uncommon), Meso F1 (uncommon)
  • Orokin Cell

Building it in the Foundry costs 25,000 credits and takes 3 days.

Stats

  • Health: 365
  • Shields: 270
  • Armor: 315
  • Energy: 200
  • Sprint speed: 1

Abilities

  • Spores: Inflict a target with a pox of Corrosive spores. Spread spores to nearby enemies by destroying them or killing their host. The longer the Spore spreads, its damage will increase.
  • Molt: Shedding her skin like a snake, Saryn leaves a decoy behind to draw fire from enemies.
  • Toxic Lash: While active, attacks deal additional Toxin Damage; this effect is doubled for Melee Strikes. Instantly burst spores when attacking afflicted enemies.
  • Miasma: Release a poisonous miasma that deals Viral Damage to enemies in range. Foes afflicted by spores are more susceptible to the mist.
