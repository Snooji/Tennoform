Harrow Prime

Warframe · Prime · Vaulted

How to get Harrow Prime

  • Blueprint comes from Axi K6 relic (uncommon), Lith B11 relic (uncommon), Lith P6 relic (uncommon), Lith S15 relic (uncommon), Neo G5 relic (uncommon), Neo L3 relic (uncommon), Neo N21 relic (uncommon).
  • It is vaulted: its relics no longer drop, so get them from Prime Resurgence or by trading.

Parts and resources

  • Harrow Prime Neuroptics Blueprint: relics Axi A16 (uncommon), Axi S12 (uncommon), Axi T11 (uncommon), Axi T8 (uncommon), Lith B10 (uncommon), Lith R3 (uncommon)
  • Harrow Prime Chassis Blueprint: relics Axi N10 (uncommon), Axi S15 (uncommon), Meso G3 (uncommon), Meso N13 (uncommon), Meso O5 (uncommon), Meso P11 (uncommon)
  • Harrow Prime Systems Blueprint: relics Axi H7 (rare), Lith H10 (rare), Lith H3 (rare), Lith H4 (rare), Lith H5 (rare), Lith H7 (rare)
  • Orokin Cell ×5

Building it in the Foundry costs 25,000 credits and takes 3 days.

Stats

  • Health: 270
  • Shields: 640
  • Armor: 185
  • Energy: 140
  • Sprint speed: 1

Abilities

  • Condemn: Cast a wave of energy that chains them where they stand. Each enemy held reinforces Harrow's shields.
  • Penance: Sacrifice Shields to boost Reload Speed, and Fire Rate while converting damage inflicted on enemies into health for Harrow and nearby allies.
  • Thurible: Channel Harrow's energy into the Thurible to generate a buff. Once finished, kill enemies to bestow nearby allies with bursts of energy. The more energy channeled the greater the reward for each kill. Weak Point kills produce extra energy.
  • Covenant: Protect nearby allies with an energy force that absorbs all damage and converts it to a Critical Chance bonus for all those under the Covenant. Weak Point hits are amplified even further.
