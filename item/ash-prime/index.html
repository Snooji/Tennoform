Ash Prime

Warframe · Prime · Vaulted

How to get Ash Prime

  • Blueprint comes from Axi I3 relic (uncommon), Lith S3 relic (uncommon), Lith V7 relic (uncommon), Meso C1 relic (uncommon), Meso S3 relic (uncommon).
  • It is vaulted: its relics no longer drop, so get them from Prime Resurgence or by trading.

Parts and resources

  • Ash Prime Neuroptics Blueprint: relics Axi N2 (uncommon), Meso N2 (uncommon), Meso P12 (uncommon), Meso V4 (uncommon), Neo A4 (uncommon), Neo N3 (uncommon)
  • Ash Prime Chassis Blueprint: relics Axi B1 (uncommon), Axi K12 (uncommon), Lith V8 (uncommon), Meso V2 (uncommon), Neo N4 (uncommon)
  • Ash Prime Systems Blueprint: relics Axi A7 (rare), Axi N1 (rare), Neo A10 (rare), Neo N5 (uncommon)
  • Orokin Cell

Building it in the Foundry costs 25,000 credits and takes 3 days.

Stats

  • Health: 455
  • Shields: 365
  • Armor: 185
  • Energy: 100
  • Sprint speed: 1.2

Abilities

  • Shuriken: Launches a spinning blade of pain, dealing high damage and impaling enemies to walls.
  • Smoke Screen: Drops a smoke bomb that stuns enemies and obscures their vision, rendering Ash invisible for a short time.
  • Teleport: Teleport to a target and perform a Finisher. If the finisher kills the target, Ash regains a portion of the energy spent.
  • Blade Storm: Project fierce Shadow Clones of Ash upon groups of distant enemies. Join the fray using Teleport. The Shadow Clones can be upgraded with mods.
