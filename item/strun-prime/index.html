Strun Prime

Primary · Prime · Mastery rank 14 · Vaulted

How to get Strun Prime

  • Blueprint comes from Lith S11 relic (rare), Lith S12 relic (rare), Meso S10 relic (rare), Neo S17 relic (rare), Neo S19 relic (rare).
  • It is vaulted: its relics no longer drop, so get them from Prime Resurgence or by trading.

Parts and resources

  • Strun Prime Barrel: relics Axi N12 (uncommon), Axi N8 (uncommon), Axi P4 (uncommon), Lith B10 (uncommon), Lith H5 (uncommon), Lith N12 (uncommon)
  • Strun Prime Receiver: relics Axi I2 (uncommon), Axi T8 (uncommon), Meso G3 (uncommon), Meso P10 (uncommon), Neo C3 (uncommon), Neo D5 (uncommon)
  • Strun Prime Stock: relics Axi K10 (uncommon), Lith G5 (uncommon), Lith H6 (uncommon), Meso D7 (uncommon), Meso M5 (uncommon), Meso S11 (uncommon)
  • Orokin Cell ×10

Building it in the Foundry costs 20,000 credits and takes 12 hours.
