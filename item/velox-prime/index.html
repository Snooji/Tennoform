Velox Prime

Secondary · Prime · Mastery rank 13 · Vaulted

How to get Velox Prime

  • Blueprint comes from Axi F2 relic (uncommon), Axi S18 relic (uncommon), Lith B12 relic (uncommon), Lith D7 relic (uncommon), Meso N17 relic (uncommon), Neo C6 relic (uncommon), Neo D10 relic (uncommon).
  • It is vaulted: its relics no longer drop, so get them from Prime Resurgence or by trading.

Parts and resources

  • Velox Prime Barrel: relics Axi V12 (rare), Meso V10 (rare), Meso V11 (rare), Meso V14 (rare), Meso V16 (rare), Meso V9 (rare)
  • Velox Prime Receiver: relics Axi A20 (uncommon), Axi A23 (uncommon), Axi P8 (uncommon), Lith T12 (uncommon), Lith Z4 (uncommon), Meso V12 (uncommon)
  • Orokin Cell ×10

Building it in the Foundry costs 15,000 credits and takes 12 hours.
